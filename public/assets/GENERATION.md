# 手柄素材生成记录

使用内置 imagegen 工具生成，要求透明底、正面视角，用于看板。

## Switch Pro

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: a transparent PNG sprite for a small Windows game-controller dashboard. A single controller, perfectly straight front view, orthographic, no rotation, no hands, no cable, no stand, no perspective tilt. Neutral thumb sticks and unpressed buttons. Realistic polished 3D studio product rendering, clean edges and soft neutral lighting. Entire controller visible, centered, fills most of a landscape 4:3 canvas with clear transparent margin. True transparent background, no ground shadow, no checkerboard, no labels outside the controller, no watermark. Buttons and thumb-stick caps must be crisp and spatially separate so software can overlay highlights and move each thumb cap. Subject: Nintendo Switch Pro Controller in the user's white-grip style: dark smoky charcoal center body, white grips, asymmetric sticks (left high, right low), cross-shaped D-pad lower left, right-side Nintendo button layout X top, A right, B bottom, Y left. Small minus and plus buttons, round Home button and square Capture button. No brand wordmark on the body. Keep the familiar Nintendo Switch Pro silhouette and proportions.
